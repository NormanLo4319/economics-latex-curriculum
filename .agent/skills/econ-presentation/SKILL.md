# Economics Presentation Design & Generation Guide

## Core Requirement: Depth & Structure (6-8+ Slides per Topic/Chapter)

  - **Minimum Slide Count:** Every presentation deck must contain at least 6 to 8 comprehensive slides per topic/chapter to ensure thorough, step-by-step progression through economic concepts, mathematical derivations, graphical models, and real-world examples.
  - **Pedagogical Flow (Step-by-Step):**
    1.  Title & Learning Objectives
    2.  Intuition & Real-World Framing (Story)
    3.  Geometric & Visual Framework (Graph with clear axis labels and curve shifts)
    4.  Mathematical / Algebraic Derivation (Step-by-step equations wrapped in LaTeX)
    5.  Step-by-Step Worked Example / Numerical Problem
    6.  Policy Implications & Real-World Case Study
    7.  Common Student Pitfalls ("Clear It Up")
    8.  Summary & Knowledge Check

## Visual & Technical Standards

  - **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 widescreen layout.
  - **Color Taxonomy:** Slate Blue (`#1E293B`) for headers/axes, Royal Blue (`#2563EB`) for Demand/Benefits, Crimson (`#DC2626`) for Supply/Costs, Terracotta (`#C2410C`) for Equilibrium/DWL/Highlights.
  - **Precision:** No truncated labels, unpadded text, or overlapping vector shapes.
